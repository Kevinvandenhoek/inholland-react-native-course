# Weekweather

Plan your outdoor week: see the weather for the next seven days in the cities you care about.

## Core action

Pick a city and see which days are dry.

## Features

1. Search a city by name.
2. See the next seven days for a city: temperature, rain and wind per day.
3. Tap a day to see the weather per hour.
4. Save a city to My places, and remove it again.
5. Share the forecast of one day with a friend.

## Data

- **API**: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs), no key. Cities come from the [geocoding API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/geocoding-api).
- **Stored on the phone**: My places (name, latitude, longitude, date added), so they are still there after a restart.

## Native feature

Share, on the day screen.
